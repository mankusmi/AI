Requirements Visualiser

Edit a requirements set and see it as a priority board, a dependency map and a goal traceability matrix. Gaps and conflicts are flagged as you type. Sample data is loaded; replace it with your own.

Import, export and reset

Copy the JSON to keep a backup, or paste your own requirements and load them. Fields: id, title, description, type, priority (Must, Should, Could, Wont), status, stakeholder, goals (list), effort (points), depends (list of ids).

DevOps integration (Azure DevOps, Jira, GitHub)

This page cannot call your tracker directly. Export a query from Azure DevOps or Jira as CSV (or JSON) and paste it to link work items, or export requirements in the format your tracker imports. Work items are matched to requirements by an ID such as REQ-03 in the title or tags.

Link work items from your tracker

Recognised columns: ID or Key, Title or Summary, State or Status, Assigned To or Assignee, Iteration Path or Sprint, Tags or Labels, Story Points, Build, Environment, PR.
