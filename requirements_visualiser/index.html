Requirements Visualiser

Edit a requirements set and see it as a priority board, a dependency map and a goal traceability matrix. Gaps and conflicts are flagged as you type. Sample data is loaded; replace it with your own.

Import, export and reset

Copy the JSON to keep a backup, or paste your own requirements and load them. Fields: id, title, description, type, priority (Must, Should, Could, Wont), status, stakeholder, goals (list), effort (points), depends (list of ids).
